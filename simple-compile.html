Simple Compile

Open a Simple Minutes (or Minutes) CSV export and get readable minutes back — Minutes Maker prose, an SHMHA Minutes Book submission, or a terse Simple List — ready to copy, paste, and email. No editing, no saved project: open a file, get your text, done.

Need to fix a name, reorder songs, or manage tunebook sources first? Use the full Minutes app instead — everything made here can also be opened there.

1. Open a file

A Master CSV from Minutes, or a CSV exported directly from Simple Minutes — leaderless files work exactly as they're meant to here.

Click to choose a file or drop a CSV here.

2. Choose the output

Per-song & summary options

Song title, ALL CAPS, and the summary line also apply to the Simple List; everything else here is Minutes-Maker-only. SHMHA Minutes Book Submission always follows its own fixed format regardless of any of these.

Wording & presentation (optional)

Applies to the narrative regardless of type — patterned on real published minutes rather than one flattened formula.

3. Copy, download, or email

Shown as it appears in every export except the SHMHA submission — Work Codes, ordinary punctuation.
